STREAMSHIELD OBS BRANDING

Included files
- StreamShield_Full_Overlay.png — full stacked logo for scenes, starting/ending screens, and larger placements.
- StreamShield_Horizontal_Overlay.png — horizontal logo for lower thirds and small branded panels.
- StreamShield_Shield_Badge.png — shield-only badge for a subtle stream-corner placement.
- StreamShield_Brand_Kit_Preview.png — visual brand preview.

OBS setup
1. Open OBS Studio.
2. In Sources, click + and choose Image.
3. Select one of the PNG files above.
4. Resize and position it where you want on your scene.
5. Lock the source after placement so it is not moved accidentally.

These overlay PNGs use transparency. Keep the original aspect ratio when resizing.
