StreamShield Privacy

StreamShield is a defensive Kick-streaming security tool. OAuth tokens are encrypted locally on the streamer's computer. The cloud OAuth broker processes authorization codes and refresh tokens transiently for token exchange or refresh and does not persist streamer OAuth tokens.

Signed Kick events are buffered for reliability. Public chat text expires after 15 minutes and whole webhook event rows expire after 24 hours.

StreamShield does not collect passwords, stream keys, viewer IP addresses, private messages or invasive device fingerprints.

The application includes Disconnect Kick and Delete My StreamShield Data controls.
