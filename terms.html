StreamShield Terms & Safe Use

StreamShield is defensive-only software for detecting suspicious engagement and assisting streamers with supported moderation actions.

It must not be used to generate or inflate viewers, followers, chat activity, subscriptions, raids or other engagement, to bypass Kick protections, or to interfere with monetization or platform enforcement.

Detection scores are indicators, not proof of who caused suspicious activity. Destructive moderation should remain conservative and user-authorized.

Kick retains control over enforcement, monetization and Partner Program decisions. No third-party tool can guarantee a platform outcome.
