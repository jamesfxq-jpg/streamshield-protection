Terms & Safe Use

StreamShield is beta software for suspicious-engagement monitoring, supported moderation, and incident evidence. The streamer remains responsible for the moderation settings and actions they authorize.

Defensive purpose

Use StreamShield only for authorized channel protection. Do not use it to generate or inflate views, follows, chat, subscriptions, raids, or other engagement; bypass KICK protections; access private systems; or interfere with advertising, monetization, or platform enforcement.

Platform limits

StreamShield cannot prevent platform-side fake follows, guarantee removal of artificial viewers, or obtain KICK viewer IP addresses. Supported moderation depends on KICK’s available interfaces and the permissions granted to the app. KICK retains authority over platform enforcement, monetization, and Partner Program decisions.

StreamShield is not a KICK service and does not guarantee account protection, detection of every incident, or a particular platform outcome.

Moderation and false positives

Detection scores indicate unusual patterns; they are not proof of abuse or identity. Legitimate raids, promotions, shared networks, and changing connection addresses can produce misleading signals. Review the reasons for an alert and act conservatively.

Automatic user timeouts are separately opt-in. Follow Shield records suspicious follow bursts and does not ban users solely because of follow velocity. Network Protection and exact-network automatic bans also require streamer opt-in. First-party browser-token matches require moderator review by default.

Only use targeted verification with viewers you are authorized to moderate. Explain why verification is requested and provide a way to contact your moderation team. Do not represent a network match or browser-token match as a permanent hardware identity.

Recovery and evidence

Recovery Center reverses supported recent actions; not every action or consequence is reversible. Network blocks and KICK account bans have separate controls. Removing a StreamShield network block does not automatically remove an existing KICK account ban.

Reports document recorded activity, detection reasons, and actions. A SHA-256 evidence seal can help check whether a report file has changed. It does not establish who caused suspicious traffic or independently verify every event.

Beta availability

Beta features may change and can be interrupted by local, network, backend, or KICK service failures. Check readiness before streaming and keep manual moderation available. The Windows beta is not yet commercially code signed; keep Windows security protections enabled.

Privacy and branding

Read Privacy & Data Controls before enabling optional verification. Use verified connection information only for legitimate channel moderation.

The supplied StreamShield PNGs are available for showing StreamShield branding in your scenes. A static logo is branding and does not indicate live monitoring status or a KICK endorsement.
